Activity 9 · Question 9

Reading Detective

Ontario C2.6

Attempts: 0

Curriculum focus: Identify the main idea and the details that support it.

Read a story and find what it tells you.

📚 Tractors Day

Mira and Jonah went to the forest-edge farm to reach the seed garden. A parked tractor blocked the narrow footpath. Its tire marks crossed the lane shown on their old map, so they kept away from the machinery. Mira noticed that a fresh walking-route sign pointed around the field. They followed it beside a fence. At a gate, the safe path was hard to see, and the machinery lane looked like a shortcut. Jonah found a smaller walking marker on the gate. It pointed along the visitor path, past rows of seedlings, and finally to the seed garden. They had used the sign and the gate marker instead of guessing where the old footpath might reopen.

Which newer clue helped them choose?

📚 Tractors Day
