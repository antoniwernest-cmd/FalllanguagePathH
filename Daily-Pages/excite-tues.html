Activity 9 · Question 9

Reading Detective

Ontario C2.6

Attempts: 0

Curriculum focus: Identify the main idea and the details that support it.

Read a story and find what it tells you.

📚 Bike Ride Day

Mira and Jonah went to the woodland bike trail to reach the lookout. Rain had left the lower path muddy. A wheel could catch in the deep ruts, even though their printed route still showed that trail. Mira noticed that fresh orange arrows marked a higher path. They checked that the arrows stayed inside the marked riding area and began to climb. Near the top, loose gravel slowed them again. They walked their bicycles carefully until the ground grew firm. From the lookout, they could see the muddy path below. The fresh arrows and the condition of the ground had helped them choose a route for that morning.

Which newer clue helped them choose?
