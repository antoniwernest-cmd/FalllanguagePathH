Fall Language Path 1 — five themed learning days

Open index.html in a current web browser. Choose Entry Way, Excite, Inspire,
or Explorer, then choose Monday Aquarium, Tuesday Bike Ride, Wednesday Tractors,
Thursday Boating, or Friday Cave Explore.

Each day is one scrollable page with 12 numbered activities, a glossary, slow
recorded narration, saved attempts, and a downloadable assessment PDF. Keep
this folder together so the day pages can load their recordings and scripts.
